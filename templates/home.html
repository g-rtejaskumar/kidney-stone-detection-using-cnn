How it works

Three steps to a diagnosis

A real sequence, from scan to answer.

01
Upload a scan

Drop in a kidney ultrasound or CT image. JPG or PNG, up to any size the model can read.

02
CNN analyzes it

The image is resized to 224×224 and passed through a 9-layer convolutional network trained on 10,000 labeled scans.

03
Read the result

A clear verdict with a confidence score — stone present or not — returned in under a second.

The model

Trained on real kidney imaging data

NephroScan is a custom convolutional neural network built with TensorFlow / Keras. It was trained for 5 epochs on a dataset of 10,000 kidney images with rotation, zoom and flip augmentation, then evaluated on a held-out 20% validation split.
